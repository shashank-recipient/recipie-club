Four ways to be connected

Partner

You and a neighbour point customers to each other. “Gym, then breakfast.” The strongest link in the Club.

Follow

Keep an eye on a neighbour’s news and events. A light link that sends a few customers your way.

Neutral

No link yet. You’re still in the same neighbourhood and benefit when it gets busier.

Rival

Someone who sells what you sell. In the Club, rivals can still team up to grow demand for both, like a neighbourhood coffee walk.
